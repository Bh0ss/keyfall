# Bundled song notices

The compositions are in the public domain. The MusicXML files are Keyfall arrangements, except where listed here.

## chopin-nocturne-op72-1.musicxml

Chopin, Nocturne in E minor, Op. 72 No. 1 (posth.). The composition is public domain.

This one file is an adaptation licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Pitches, rhythms and ornaments were verified against, and in places taken from, the Mutopia Project typesetting by Benjamin Esham (CC BY-SA 4.0, https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=509) of the Joseffy / G. Schirmer 1915 edition (public domain). License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. The licence applies to this file only, not to the app code.

## schumann-melody-op68-1.musicxml and schumann-happy-farmer.musicxml

Schumann, Melody (Album for the Young, Op. 68 No. 1) and The Happy Farmer (Op. 68 No. 10). The compositions are public domain.

These two files are adaptations licensed CC BY-SA 2.5, converted from the Mutopia Project typesettings by Philippe Hezaine of the Peters edition (https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=647 and ?id=659). License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/. The licence applies to these two files only, not to the app code.

## Derived from public-domain Mutopia Project files

These were converted from the Mutopia MIDI exports of public-domain typesettings (no attribution required, given here for provenance):

- clair-de-lune.musicxml: Debussy, typeset by Keith OHara from E. Fromont (1905).
- minuet-in-g.musicxml: Petzold, Mutopia anna-magdalena-04 (Bach-Gesellschaft edition).
- fur-elise.musicxml: Beethoven, Mutopia fur_Elise_WoO59 (Breitkopf & Haertel 1888).
- greensleeves.musicxml: Traditional, Mutopia GreensleevesAcc.

Also converted from Mutopia MIDI exports of public-domain typesettings:

- bach-musette-in-d.musicxml: Bach, Musette in D BWV Anh. 126, Mutopia anna-magdalena-22 (id 79), typeset by Allen Garvin, Bach-Gesellschaft edition.
- tchaikovsky-old-french-song.musicxml: Tchaikovsky, Old French Song Op. 39 No. 16, Mutopia id 2080, typeset by David McNamara from Schirmer 1904.
- burgmuller-arabesque.musicxml: Burgmueller, Arabesque Op. 100 No. 2, Mutopia id 203, typeset by Bas Wassink, Collection Litolff (placed in the public domain by the typesetter).
- bach-prelude-in-c.musicxml: Bach, Prelude in C BWV 846, Mutopia wtk1-prelude1 (id 5), typeset by Tobias Erbsland.
- clementi-sonatina-op36-1-mvt1.musicxml: Clementi, Sonatina Op. 36 No. 1, 1st movement, Mutopia sonatina-1 (id 804), typeset by Brian D. Rude from the Schirmer Sonatina Album 1893.
- joplin-the-entertainer.musicxml: Joplin, The Entertainer, Mutopia id 263, typeset by Chris Sawer from the 1902 original edition.

## Folk and traditional arrangements

Original Keyfall arrangements of public-domain tunes. Other recordings were used to check pitches only; nothing was copied:

- row-row-row-your-boat.musicxml: pitch order checked against Wikimedia Commons "Row Your Boat round.mid" (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- london-bridge.musicxml: standard tune, no reference file.
- frere-jacques.musicxml: checked against Commons "Frere Jacques (pg).mid" (CC0).
- yankee-doodle.musicxml: checked against Commons "Yankee Doodle (pg).mid" (CC0).
- oh-susanna.musicxml: Stephen Foster, 1848; checked against a Commons pentatonic melody MIDI (public domain).
- happy-birthday.musicxml: Hill sisters, 1893; public domain in the US after the 2015 ruling in Good Morning to You Productions v. Warner/Chappell (settlement approved 2016). Instrumental melody only; checked against a Commons MIDI (public domain).
- amazing-grace.musicxml: New Britain; pitch and rhythm checked against Commons "Amazing grace.mid" (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- brahms-lullaby.musicxml: Brahms, 1868, transposed to C; checked against Commons "Guten Abend, gut Nacht (Wiegenlied).mid" (CC0).
- auld-lang-syne.musicxml: traditional Scottish, transposed to C; checked against Commons "Auld Lang Syne (three-four).mid" (CC BY 4.0, Rabanus Flavus).
- scarborough-fair.musicxml: traditional, in A natural minor; checked against Commons "Scarborough-fair.mid" (public domain).
